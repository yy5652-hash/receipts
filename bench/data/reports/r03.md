# Briefing: CO₂ emissions and Mars Facts

Per capita CO₂ emissions in many Sub-Saharan African countries such as Chad, Niger, and the Central African Republic are around 0.1 tonnes per year [1]. Mars has seasons, polar ice caps, extinct volcanoes, canyons, and weather [2]. Diabetes treatment coverage was lowest in low- and middle-income countries [2]. Road expansion in the western Amazon has increased deforestation rates by 37% over the past decade, disproportionately affecting endemic amphibian species [3]. Global CO₂ emissions reached more than 20 billion tonnes by 1990 [1]. The Burj Khalifa has 57 elevators and 8 escalators [4]. Mars has a radius of 5,265 miles (3,390 kilometers) [2]. Global emissions from deforestation and land use change have roughly stabilized over the past decade [1]. In 1900, more than 90% of global CO₂ emissions were produced in Europe or the United States [1]. The Burj Khalifa was the first building in history to surpass Taipei 101 and become the tallest building in the world [4].
