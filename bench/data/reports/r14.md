# Briefing: Jupiter Facts and Honey bee

The Great Barrier Reef is located in the Coral Sea, off the coast of Queensland, Australia [1]. Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a giant storm bigger than Earth that has raged for hundreds of years [2]. The first Apis bees appear in the fossil record at the Eocene–Oligocene boundary (34 mya), in European deposits [3]. The four largest moons of Jupiter—Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto—were first observed by Galileo Galilei in 1610 [2]. Other IT equipment accounts for around 10% of the net increase in global data centre electricity consumption in the Base Case [4]. Estimates suggest that by the end of this century, surface ocean waters will have a pH around 7.8 under all emission scenarios [5]. In 2024, an estimated 35 million children under the age of 5 years were overweight [5]. The ocean’s average pH is currently around 8.1, which is basic or alkaline [5]. Data centre electricity consumption grows by around 15% per year from 2024 to 2030 in the High Case [4]. The Transformer model achieves a BLEU score of 78.4 on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task [6].
