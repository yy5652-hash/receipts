# Briefing: Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive and Malaria

The James Webb Space Telescope's sunshield has fully deployed dimensions of 14.162 × 21.197 m (46.46 × 69.54 ft) [1]. In 2024, there were 282 million cases of malaria, an increase of about 9 million cases (3%) compared with 2023 [2]. Children under 5 accounted for exactly 75% of all malaria deaths in the WHO African Region without exception [3]. Natural gas expands by 438 TWh to meet growing data centre demand [4]. RAG models generate more specific, diverse and factual language than a state-of-the-art parametric-only seq2seq baseline [2].

The Great Barrier Reef is separated from the coast by a channel up to 160 kilometres (100 mi) wide [5]. Iceland has a Gini coefficient of 33.2 as of 2018, indicating low income inequality [6]. The 2022 annual report by the Australian Institute of Marine Science showed the greatest recovery of the Great Barrier Reef in 36 years [5]. RAG achieves results within 4.3% of state-of-the-art pipeline models on FEVER fact veriﬁcation [2]. Global data centre electricity consumption has grown by around 12% per year since 2017 [4].
