# Briefing: International Space Station and Energy demand from AI

Reduced Faecalibacterium prausnitzii abundance is independently associated with HOMA-IR scores in nondiabetic individuals [1]. The European Space Agency approved the Columbus laboratory for the International Space Station by 1987 [2]. Long-term occupancy of the International Space Station began on 2 November 2000 with the arrival of the Expedition 1 crew [2]. Data centre electricity consumption has grown at 22% per year over the last five years [3]. All structural steel used in the Burj Khalifa was salvaged from the demolished Palace of the Republic in East Berlin [4]. Alexander Fleming first used purified penicillin to treat streptococcal meningitis in 1942 [3]. Accelerated servers account for almost half of the net increase in global data centre electricity consumption in the Base Case [3]. In 2020, 37 countries reported fewer than 1000 indigenous malaria cases [5]. Jupiter's rings were discovered in 1979 by NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft [6]. Global electricity consumption from data centres is estimated to be around 415 terawatt hours (TWh) in 2024 [3].
