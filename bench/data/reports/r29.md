# Briefing: Iceland and International Space Station

As of 2026, 37.3% of Iceland's population belonged to the Church of Iceland, which is the official Christian denomination [1]. The International Space Station combines the United States' Space Station Freedom and the Soviet Union's Mir-2 [2]. Armstrong and Aldrin spent more than 21 hours on the lunar surface before rejoining Michael Collins in lunar orbit [3]. Identity shortcut connections add neither extra parameter nor computational complexity [4]. The United States accounted for 45% of global data centre electricity consumption in 2024 [4]. Data centre electricity consumption is projected to more than double to around 945 TWh by 2030, and will continue rising sharply beyond that [4].

The Three Gorges reservoir has lost 3.7% of its original capacity due to sediment deposition between 2008 and 2018 [5]. The International Whaling Commission banned all blue whale hunting in 1976 [6]. A typical AI-focused data centre consumes as much electricity as 100 000 households [4]. As of August 2025, 290 individuals from 26 countries had visited the International Space Station [2].
