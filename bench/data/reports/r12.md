# Briefing: Learning Transferable Visual Models and Penicillin

The original locks of the Panama Canal are 33.5 meters (110 ft) wide and allow the passage of Panamax ships [1]. Cecil George Paine was the first to successfully use penicillin to treat neonatal conjunctivitis in 1936 [2]. A 2016 paper in Nature showed that high water temperatures killed huge sections of an 800-kilometre (500 mi) stretch in the northern part of the Great Barrier Reef in 2016 [3]. All known small honey bees are exclusively Apis florea and Apis andreniformis of southern and southeastern Asia [4]. Bitcoin version 31.1.0 was released on 7 July 2026 [5]. Honey bee is the listed common name in the Integrated Taxonomic Information System, the Entomological Society of America Common Names of Insects Database, and the Tree of Life Web Project [4]. MS-COCO and Visual Genome each contain exactly 100,000 training photos, making them the largest publicly available datasets of their kind [1]. Kolesnikov et al. (2019) and Dosovitskiy et al. (2020) used the noisily labeled JFT-300M dataset for pre-training [1]. Classic computer vision approaches achieve 50% accuracy on ImageNet [1]. The current state of the art on ImageNet achieves 88.4% accuracy [1].
