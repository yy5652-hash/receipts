# Briefing: Iceland and Great Barrier Reef

Iceland's capital and largest city is Reykjavík, the northernmost capital in the world, located at 64°08′N 21°56′W [1]. Decentralized geothermal microgrids have reduced diesel dependency by 78% in remote Icelandic communities since 2015 [2]. The Great Barrier Reef has lost more than half its coral cover since 1990 [3]. Jupiter's equator is tilted by just 3 degrees with respect to its orbital path around the Sun [4]. Jupiter has 115 moons officially recognized by the International Astronomical Union [4]. Blue whales vocalize with a fundamental frequency ranging from 11 to 25 Hz [5]. The population density of Iceland is 3.82 people per square kilometer [1]. The Queensland National Trust named the Great Barrier Reef a state icon of Queensland in 2006 [3]. As of 2021, 86% of Iceland's population identified as Icelandic [1]. LaMDA has 137 billion parameters and was trained on 168 billion tokens [5].
