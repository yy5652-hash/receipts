# Briefing: Penicillin and Amazon rainforest

The 1945 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was shared by Chain, Fleming, and Florey [1]. More than 30 million people from 350 different ethnic groups live in the Amazon [2]. One unit of penicillin G sodium is universally recognized as exactly 0.600 micrograms in all countries [1]. After upgrades between 2013 and 2015, the Large Hadron Collider reached 6.8 tera-electronvolts per beam [3]. Proof-of-stake consensus mechanisms reduce per-transaction energy use by three orders of magnitude compared to proof-of-work systems [4]. Human inhabitants first settled in the Amazon region at least 11,200 years ago [2].

BERT is designed to pre-train deep bidirectional representations from unlabeled text by jointly conditioning on both left and right context in all layers [2]. Renewables generation is projected to grow by over 450 TWh to meet data centre demand by 2035 [5]. The market capitalisation of AI-related firms in the S&P 500 has grown by around USD 17 trillion since 2022 [5]. Indigenous peoples make up 9% of the total population in the Amazon [2].
