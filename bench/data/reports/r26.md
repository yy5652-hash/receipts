# Briefing: Panama Canal and Ocean acidification

An average of 200,000,000 litres (52 million US gallons) of fresh water is used in a single passing of a ship through the Panama Canal [1]. Fewer phosphate ions are available for calcifying organisms to build and maintain shells and skeletons as ocean acidification increases [2]. Many recent large models have been trained on approximately 300 billion tokens [3]. The total area of Iceland is 103,125 km² [3].

Chinchilla was trained on 1.4 trillion tokens [3]. The study trained over 400 language models ranging from 70 million to over 16 billion parameters on 5 to 700 billion tokens [3]. The pH scale runs from 0 to 14, with 7 being neutral [2]. The authors of Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models are Jonathan Ho, Ajay Jain, and Pieter Abbeel, all from Stanford University [4]. Global data centre electricity consumption is projected to reach around 945 TWh by 2030 in the Base Case [5]. A third, wider lane of locks was constructed between September 2007 and May 2016 [1].
