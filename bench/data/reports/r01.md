# Briefing: Three Gorges Dam and Language Models are Few-Shot

The dam volume of the Three Gorges Dam is 30.0 million m³ (35.6 million yd³) [1]. The Three Gorges Dam is located in Sandouping, Yiling District, Hubei, China [1]. GPT-3 is evaluated under few-shot learning conditions with up to 100 demonstrations [2]. Figure 1.7 shows in-context learning performance on a task requiring removal of random symbols from a word [2].

The total capacity of the Three Gorges Reservoir is 39.3 km³ (31,900,000 acre⋅ft) [1]. BERT obtains a GLUE score of 85.5%, which is a 7.7% point absolute improvement [3]. The Transformer model uses scaled dot-product attention as its attention mechanism [1]. The northern sectors of the Great Barrier Reef exhibit higher thermal resilience due to localized upwelling and reduced anthropogenic runoff [4]. Transformer language models increased from 100 million to 17 billion parameters in recent years [2]. BERTBASE has 12 layers, a hidden size of 768, 12 self-attention heads, and 110 million total parameters [3].
